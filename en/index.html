Kassora – the club cash book for iPhone

Kassora is an iPhone app that keeps a cash book for clubs and teams: income and expenses, cash counts with a signable count sheet, export as a CSV table.

Private cash boxes stay on the device and need no account. Only shared cash boxes use Sign in with Apple and are stored on a server in the EU, visible only to the team.

Kassora is free, with no ads and no tracking, in German and English.
