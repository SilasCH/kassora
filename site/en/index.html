What is Kassora? A cash book app in three steps

Kassora keeps your club's cash box on your iPhone. You record what goes in and out, count the cash and sign the record.

1. Record

Income or expense: amount, category, save.

2. Count

Count the cash, enter the amount. Kassora shows the difference.

3. Sign

The count record has two lines for signatures.

Running a club cash box: the features

Kassora does little, and does it well. Everything a treasurer needs for the cash box, nothing beyond.

Record income and expenses quickly

Amount, category, save. The cash book lists entries by receipt date, grouped by day, with a clear separation when a new cash book year begins.

  • Several cash boxes side by side, for example for the club and the summer camp
  • 64 currencies to choose from

Count the cash and sign the count record

You count the cash and enter the amount. Kassora compares it with the calculated balance. If there is a difference, you note it or book it as a separate "cash difference" entry.

  • The count record shows cash box name, date, who counted, counted and calculated side by side, and the difference
  • Two signature lines: for treasurer and auditor, or for the person taking over the role
  • Share it as text, by message or email

Yearly overview and CSV export

The table shows income, expenses and the running balance for each cash book year, with amount brought forward and totals. For filing or the audit, export the entries as a CSV file and open it in a spreadsheet.

Run it as a team

The treasurer shares the cash box and invites others. Roles: treasurer, recorder, reader. Everyone sees the same entries; whoever records without a connection syncs later. Sharing requires signing in.

Locked with Face ID

If you wish, Kassora locks the app with Face ID. Text can be enlarged a lot.

Your data stays on your device

A cash box of your own needs no sign-in and sends nothing to a server.

  • Private cash boxes stay on the device, even without internet
  • Only a shared cash box is also stored on a server in the EU, visible only to its team
  • No advertising, no tracking

Frequently asked questions about the cash book app

What is Kassora?

Kassora is a cash book app for iPhone. It keeps the cash box of a club or team: record income and expenses, count the cash, create a count record to sign.

How much does Kassora cost?

Nothing. Kassora is free and contains no advertising and no tracking.

Do I need an account?

Not for a cash box of your own. It works without sign-in and without internet. Signing in is only needed to share a cash box with a team.

Where is my data stored?

Private cash boxes stay on your device. Only a shared cash box is also stored on a server in the EU, visible only to its team.

Can I keep several cash boxes?

Yes. For example one for the club and one for the summer camp. Each cash box has one currency; 64 are available.

Can several people run the cash box?

Yes. The treasurer invites others. Each person has a role: treasurer, recorder or reader. A reader can see everything but change nothing, for example for the auditor.

What is in the count record?

Cash box name, date, who counted, the counted and the calculated cash side by side, the difference and two signature lines. You can share it as text by message or email.

Can I open the entries in a spreadsheet?

Yes. Kassora exports the entries as a spreadsheet file (CSV) that common spreadsheet applications open.

Is Kassora bookkeeping software?

No. Kassora only keeps track of the cash in the box and needs no bookkeeping knowledge.

Is there a Kassora for Android?

Not at the moment, iPhone only.
